## The structure of the Wikimedia Foundation

- **The Wikimedia Foundation** is a non-profit; it asks for donations.
- **MediaWiki** is the collaborative, quick-editing software. "Wiki" comes from the Hawaiian *wikiwiki*, meaning "quick."
- **Wikipedia** is one instance of MediaWiki. Other examples include the [Arch Linux Wiki](https://wiki.archlinux.org/), the [Gentoo Linux Wiki](https://wiki.gentoo.org/), and [Fandom](https://www.fandom.com/). There are [many others](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:MediaWiki_websites) as well.

## Lesser-known Wikimedia Foundation properties

- **[Wikifunctions](https://www.wikifunctions.org/wiki/Wikifunctions:Main_Page)** — a "Rosetta stone for programming languages"; possibly a ripoff of Rosetta Code.
- **[Wikitech](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Main_Page)** — technical resources for editors and for the Foundation itself, including a detailed picture of how Wikipedia runs (for example, this [infrastructure diagram](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/WMF_infrastructure_2022.png)).
